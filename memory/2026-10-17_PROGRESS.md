1. **Security & Authentication Layer**:

     - `POST /api/user/topup-slip`: Payment screenshot receipt upload.
     - `POST /api/user/keys/<path:key_id>/replace`: Key replacement request queue.
     - `GET /api/user/history`: Purchase and slip history.
     - `GET /api/user/leaderboard`: 7-day points leaderboard rankings.
